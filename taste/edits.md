# Edits: before, after, why

Corrections actually made, as pairs. A before and after teaches more than a score. Read each with its context; it generalises only as far as its reason does. Add yours to the project's `TASTE.md`: piece, scene, before, after, why, who said so.

## The example launch storyboard (examples/unrushed-launch), 0.4 → 0.5

- **The ending.** Before: a 6 s end card from the brandbook, crema ground, symbol, wordmark, the command and the GitHub URL, held 5 s. After: 2 s; the orange dot that carried the piece becomes the symbol's accent, the command arrives whole, the last frame holds 1.2 s with the cursor blinking; the URL moved to the post copy. Why: a held logo, tagline and URL is a web footer, and a long static ending reads as "the film gave up".
- **Transitions.** Before: four designed transitions in 45 s (a mask from the cursor, a zoom-through, a dot sweep, a dissolve into the end card), chosen to show "four kinds". After: hard cuts on motion, two designed transitions, the biggest into the payoff. Why: the quota of kinds was the skill's own rule and it produced a showreel; professionals cut on motion in almost every moment.
- **The payoff.** Before: 8 s. After: a 1 s breath on the clean frame, then 5 s. Why: the payoff needs a breath before it and must stop before it is spent.
- **The brand's graphic elements.** Before: crop marks, numbering (001), a frame ruler, mono labels and a serif italic quote allowed in video. After: allowed in print and docs only. Why: in a video they are page furniture.

## The skill itself

- **The energy table.** Before: durations in milliseconds of 400–1200 ms and "at least four transition kinds" at mid energy. After: frames from the measured medians (10–15 f at mid energy) and 1–3 designed transitions per minute. Why: the old values were slower than the whole professional sample, and a quota produces transitions without a reason.
- **The curve table.** Before: a back-out curve with an early overshoot for "playful arrivals". After: `popOver`, a late lean, once per piece. Why: measured overshoot is rare, late and small; an early bounce is the AI default.
- **The demo.** Before: "simulate the interface". After: find the real product, write a demo script of actions and responses, and never count a camera move as a step. Why: see the 2026-11-21 verdicts.
